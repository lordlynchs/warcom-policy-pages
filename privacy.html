WarCom is published by James Lynch in the United Kingdom. Privacy contact: jamesalynch@live.ca.

1. How the beta handles information

WarCom runs on your computer and connects Discord bots that you create to the voice channels you configure. WarCom does not operate a central account or voice-relay server in this beta.

The app stores your Discord server and channel IDs, display labels, bot tokens, and radio setup on your computer. It also stores callout plans and generated callout audio locally. Bot tokens are saved in local files with restrictive file permissions, but the beta does not encrypt them. Keep your computer account secure and never send a token to support.

While the service is running, it temporarily processes voice audio in memory to relay it between the channels you selected. The beta does not intentionally record or save members’ live voice conversations, and WarCom does not receive a copy of that audio on a WarCom server. Discord receives the voice traffic and bot activity needed to provide Discord voice services.

The app keeps a short operational event list in memory while running. It does not include analytics or telemetry that sends your setup or voice audio to WarCom. If you contact support, WarCom receives the information you choose to include in your message.
