1. The service

WarCom is a hosted Discord application that connects voice channels selected by a server administrator. WarCom’s voice bots join the channels authorized by the administrator, and WarCom’s hosted service relays audio between each configured pair. The Where Winds Meet callout add-on can play timed voice announcements through connected WarCom bots. The service depends on Discord, the customer’s server and permissions, the customer’s internet connection, and WarCom’s hosted infrastructure. WarCom is beta software and may contain faults or interruptions. No uptime commitment applies unless the current published plan listing expressly promises one.

2. Your account, server, and bots

You must have authority to manage the Discord server and channels you connect.
